## Blog Post Week 13

One thing presenting in a group makes obvious fast: everyone has a different idea of what the presentation should be.

<!--more-->
The fault line is always the same at it's core: technical depth vs. accessibility. Some people want to show the work, the real work, architecture and implementation details and the actual complexity of what was built. Others want to strip it down to the value proposition and get out. Neither is wrong, but getting everyone to a shared vision of what the talk is takes real coordination overhead that has nothing to do with the content itself.
We landed on a compromise. Not a perfect one, but a functional one. Keep enough technical grounding to be credible, cut enough to stay followable for a mixed room. Speaking to the professor she told us to err towards the side of explaining to people who don't have an idea of this architecture, so we will try do that instead of being technical with our contributions. Externally, i've commented on some discussion posts and will keep them updated as time goes on.
